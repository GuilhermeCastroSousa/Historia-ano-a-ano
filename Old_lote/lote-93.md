publicado: 2021-05-21 · duracao: PT27M36S
descricao: 'The History of Byzantium' is a podcast telling the story of the Roman Empire from 476 AD to 1453. In episode 20 we cover the famous Nika riots of 532AD. Resentment against the Emperor Justinian had been building for some time. When he failed to pardon two members of the Hippodrome racing fan clubs things turned nasty. The Blues and Greens united, burning down the centre of Constantinople and agitating for a new Emperor to take the throne. Justinian happened to have some loyal troops on hand and eventually turned them on his own people. Professor Ivanov is a Russian scholar who has been studying Byzantium for many decades. He currently works in the Institute of Oriental and Ancient Studies at the National Research University “Higher School of Economics” in Moscow. He has written several books about Byzantium as well as contributing hundreds of articles to the field. His areas of study include Byzantine religious missions, the cultural influence of Byzantium on the Rus', Holy Fools and Constantinople itself.
