### xg-XY-5jEZc · Lucius Licinius Varro Murena, Executed 22 BCE
publicado: 2021-01-01 · duracao: PT42M47S
descricao: While not quite a member of Augustus' inner circle, Varro Murena was very much a political insider and partisan supporter of Augustus during his rise to power. However, following one ill-advised, unscripted remark, Varro Murena found himself not only out of favor with the emperor but also suspected of engaging in or inciting a conspiracy. The death of Varro Murena and the events surrounding it played a key role in the so-called Second Settlement of 23 BCE, where Augustus further defined and refined his position within the state. Varro Murena's death also provides a striking illustration of the decisiveness, cold calculation, and utter ruthlessness of Augustus at the height of his power.
