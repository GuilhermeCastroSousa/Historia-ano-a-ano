But what is this lesser known demonological literature? Let’s explore several of these now obscure specialized demonological treatises - from legal, theological, even an epic poem - that produced the witch and witchcraft prior to the Malleus. Maleficarum. ### tKm1i09RpF4 · The First Book of Alchemy You Should Study
publicado: 2026-09-18 · duracao: PT37M32S
descricao: Of the occult sciences, alchemy is perhaps one of the least directly accessible to modern readers. Texts of alchemy are especially difficult to follow for a host of reasons: they assume fundamentally different physical and metaphysical structures and processes about reality than those of modernity, their language is couched in codes and symbolism and the texts are often doing as much to intentionally obscure their message as convey it. Hence, alchemy - unlike astrology and even magic - remains among the most obscure elements of western esotericism. But, not every alchemical text is marked by such extreme obscurity. Indeed, a work ascribed to none less than Albertus Magnus - the Libellus de Alchemia (the little book on alchemy) also known as the Semita Recta (the right path) - is one of the most complete and coherent volumes of both alchemical theory and practice from the Middle Ages. So, if you’ve ever wanted a truly comprehensive, yet brief and approachable, introduction to medieval alchemy this is the volume for you. Let’s work through one of the most popular alchemical handbooks of the middle ages. Consider Supporting Esoterica!
