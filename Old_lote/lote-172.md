In fact, most battles were won or lost based on this cohesion rather than casualties. How it works: Each unit will now have a Unit Leader that serves as the anchor of the unit and to which the other soldiers of that unit are magnetized to. The level of cohesion of a unit dictates how efficiently these soldiers follow the orders of the leader.
