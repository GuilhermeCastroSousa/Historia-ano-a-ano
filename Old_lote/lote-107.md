People have written whole books on very limited aspects of this topic. Obviously, we can’t cover every detail of dueling, so we will make use of a technique known as ‘Ideal Type’. This term is most closely associated with the great German sociologist Max Weber. For Weber, looking for essential characteristics and elements of a given phenomenon was key to understanding social and cultural realities.
